raise your hands to raise the roof
Right hand — melody: height picks the note, left ↔ right sweeps the filter, pinch sets volume, fist swaps the synth
Left hand — drums: raise fingers to toggle kick/snare/hihat/clap, hand height sets the tempo, fist swaps the beat

Hand Gesture Arpeggiator

Right hand plays the melody — raise it to raise the pitch, move it sideways to sweep the filter, pinch to set the volume, make a fist to change the synth.

Left hand plays the drums — lift fingers to toggle drums, raise the hand to speed up the tempo, make a fist to change the beat.
